HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.4 - November 2026

ROLLING DICE
- The dice window shows one number on each weapon and skill row. Press "Why these numbers" for the reasons, the same as on your sheet.
- A melee weapon's damage reads as one number with Brawn added, on your sheet and in the dice window.
- A character with cybernetic eyes or a brain implant gets the same dice in the dice window as on the sheet.

FOR GMS
- An NPC's weapon in the library shows the dice its qualities add, the same as the encounter's roll.

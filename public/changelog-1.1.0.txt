HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.0 - November 2026

CHANGE YOUR CHARACTER
- Modify this Character replaces Edit in creator: change your species, career, specializations, characteristics, skills, talents, Force powers and motivations from one page, without going through the creator again.
- Add a talent from outside your trees, from Modify this Character.
- If your character changes on another device while Modify this Character is open, saving keeps that change. If you both changed the same thing, the page asks which to keep.

YOUR SAVED WORK
- If you edit while signed out and the same thing changed on your account, signing back in now asks which to keep, instead of dropping the older edit.
- The which-copy question shows each side's own time.
- The question about your book choices no longer says both sides match when they don't.

LOADING
- In the player app, a part of a page that stops arriving shows Try again after 30 seconds, instead of a blank page.
- Try again on the Ship Yard no longer leaves a black page.

YOUR SHEET
- A saved Force power you haven't edited no longer says you and the book both changed it, and Take the Book's sticks.
- Items in your pack show their dice symbols, not raw codes.
- An item saved as your own copy shows What it does and Source.

GEAR, SHIPS AND NPCS
- 348 spelling fixes in ship and attachment text, each checked against the book.
- 142 NPCs corrected against their books.
- Binders ask for the book's difficulty.

- Many smaller fixes.

HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.8 - November 2026

CAMPAIGNS
- Holocron remembers your table.
- GMs: once you've opened a campaign's table, your home screen offers OPEN TABLE for it. One tap opens it again, and players you let in before sit down without a code.
- Players: after your first join, your home screen offers JOIN for that table. One tap puts you back in, with nothing to type and no wait to be let in.
- The Campaigns tile counts the XP offers WAITING for you.
- GMs: FORGET a remembered player, and next time they knock to be let in.
- GMs: a campaign lists its sessions. Start a NEW SESSION, or delete one.

THE SHOP
- Your wish list follows you to your other devices when you are signed in.

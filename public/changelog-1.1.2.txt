HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.2 - November 2026

ROLLING DICE
- On your sheet, tap the dice beside a roll to roll it.
- After a roll, the dice window's Difficulty shows the difficulty you rolled.

GETTING AROUND
- Back closes Route, the Hyperspace Run, the planet sheet and search, and keeps the GM map open.
- Pop-up cards keep the keyboard inside them, close with Escape, and put you back where you were.
- A landing tile's line shows once, not twice.
- Active Sources uses the same paper look as the rest of the site.

GEAR AND NPCS
- 48 NPC weapons roll the skill their book prints.
- Gear, weapon and armour text uses the book's commas and hyphens.

FOR GMS
- The NPC bench is now called Encounter everywhere.
- Reload any GM tab left open from before this update before you save an encounter.

- Many smaller fixes.

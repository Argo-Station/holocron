HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.0.0 - November 2026

MAKE A CHARACTER
- Take extra Obligation for more starting XP. The XP is added, and the step shows your new total.
- Your Morality strength and weakness show on your sheet.
- The step list names your game line's own step: Obligation, Duty or Morality.
- You can't finish a character without a species and a career.
- Renegade Form and Dedication ask which characteristic they raise.
- The review screen shows your talents' bonus dice and the right wound threshold.
- Quick Build no longer gives a Mystic a free skill rank.
- Quick Build on desktop shows how to take a loadout.
- On a phone, buying skill ranks scrolls to every skill.
- On a wide screen, the Obligation and Gear steps no longer hide the top of a card.
- OggDude files: credits, backstory and motivations travel both ways, and imported XP matches the file.
- Your characters list shows the right count, and the home screen shows names, not codes.

PLAY AT THE TABLE
- An attack roll shows the damage its hit deals.
- At a roll, talent and gear bonuses say when they apply, and say so when the app can't apply one for you.
- A blaster set to stun only offers short range.
- Record a contact network with your allies, and roll it from your sheet or from advancement.
- Your phone and your desk keep each other's changes. An edit on one no longer erases the other's.
- Printed sheets keep their dice symbols and include Force powers, encumbrance and backstory.
- Phone buttons are bigger, and the sources window closes with Escape.

ADVANCE
- A locked signature-ability upgrade says why it's locked.
- Commit only shows on a Force power you've upgraded to allow it.
- Dagoyan and Findsmen mentor discounts on Force power upgrades apply.

GEAR AND THE STORE
- Buy a weapon and an attachment together. Both prices come off your credits.
- Your sheet's weapon card names the attachment fitted to it.
- Crafting: tap an improvement to spend your advantages, and the bench moves on to the result.
- The craft bench only reports a flaw when one is recorded, and a failed craft no longer offers results to spend.
- Buy trade goods in the store and stow them in your ship's hold.
- With two ships, the store counter names each ship's hold.
- Lightsabers count their crystal against their hard points, and the attachment list only offers parts that fit.
- Implants work: an Adrenal Implant gives its talent and Implant Armor adds soak.
- Weapon qualities show their proper names.

SHIPS
- Seat your character as your ship's pilot and read their Piloting pool from the pilot's seat.
- Record Evasive Maneuvers on your ship in a space fight.
- Ship data matches the books: crew seats, passengers, vehicle types and weapon types.
- A Force die committed to Agility counts in a ship gunner's pool.

FOR THE GM
- The galaxy map's planet, region and world names are easy to read.

- Many smaller fixes.

HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.10 - November 2026

ROLLING DICE
- The dice your sheet works out for a roll are now a suggestion. ADJUST opens every die in the roll: add or take off any of them, then roll. Suggested puts the sheet's dice back.
- The count of trained skills over the Skills list in the dice window now counts every trained skill.
- On a phone, the Roll button stays on the skill's name line however many dice the roll has. Dice that don't fit go on their own line under it.

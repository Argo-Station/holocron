HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.3 - November 2026

FOR GMS
- Encounters has its own screen: a new ENCOUNTERS plate on the GM page.
- Run a fight there: start it, the turn order, the Destiny pool, an NPC's weapon dice straight into the dice window, damage and critical injuries, and an end-of-fight summary.
- Your saved encounters open in it; old ones show as not started.
- The NPC library's ENCOUNTERS tab moves to that screen; the library reads BROWSE · CUSTOM NPC · RECENT.

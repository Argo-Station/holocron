HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.7 - November 2026

CAMPAIGNS
- Online campaigns are on for everyone.
- GMs: your home screen has a CAMPAIGNS plate. Make a campaign, open a table with a code, and let your players in.
- Players: your home screen has a Campaigns tile. Join a table from your phone with the code or the link.
- At the table you see your party. The GM sees each player's live fight card and can send XP, which each player adds.

FOR GMS
- In your custom NPCs, an "In N encounters" button opens that encounter in the encounter manager.
- On an encounter card, pressing the conditions "+n" shows the folded conditions and "+ ADD".
- An encounter card's menu (Move up, Move down, Remove) can be pressed. It used to open behind the wound plate.

RULEBOOK PAGES
- 30 faction and planet entries cite the right book page.

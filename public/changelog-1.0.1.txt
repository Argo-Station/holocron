HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.0.1 - November 2026

YOUR SAVED WORK
- Map pins, custom NPCs, ship changes and your book choices no longer overwrite a change made on another device or tab.
- Text you type on your sheet, the NPC bench or a ship's log is kept when a save is going through.
- An automatic update no longer asks "which copy do you keep?" A real clash between two devices still asks.
- Character files has Back up everything.

LOADING
- The first screen opens faster on a slow phone.
- If part of a page can't download, the page says so and offers Try again.

YOUR SHEET
- Bought a Reward or contact network by mistake? Remove it and get its XP back.
- Force symbols are drawn the same way in the dice app, on the sheet and on NPCs.

GEAR AND THE STORE
- 485 spelling fixes in gear, weapon and armor text, each checked against the book.
- Yaladai Spice and other items show their dice symbols, not raw codes.

SHIPS
- Five hull mounts, including the VT-49's quad lasers, show their book's qualities.

FOR THE GM
- Open a part fitted to a player's ship and see its mods.

AROUND THE SITE
- Many lists have striped rows, so they're easier to read.
- Back closes a panel you opened from a menu, and stays on the screen.

- Many smaller fixes.

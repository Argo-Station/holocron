HOLOCRON - WHAT'S NEW

1.1.9 - November 2026

EVERY PAGE
- Holocron's pages are smaller to download, so they open faster, most of all on a phone or a slow connection.

ROLLING DICE
- Pressing a skill's dice on your sheet now opens the dice window at that skill, not at the top.

FOR GMS
- On a computer, PREP in a new, empty encounter opens full size instead of as a thin strip, so your first NPC search shows its results straight away.

ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
- Groundwork for running Holocron as a program on your own computer, to host a table on your home Wi-Fi.

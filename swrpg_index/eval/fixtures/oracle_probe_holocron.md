Game Master Holocron's hull templates table lists the templates a character can craft, each with a
material price, a rarity, a Mechanics check and a crafting time per silhouette. A Sleek Carapace
has a material price of 450 credits at rarity 3 and takes an Average Mechanics check, 24 hours per
silhouette (source: Game Master Holocron corpus p.57 — printed page uncalibrated). The other
templates on that table are Expanded Capacity Holds, Lightly Armored Hull, Deflective Plating and
Combat Plating (source: Game Master Holocron corpus p.57 — printed page uncalibrated).

This book's printed page is not calibrated, so these citations are admissible in replies, gates and

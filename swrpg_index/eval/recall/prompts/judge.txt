You grade answers given by a rules assistant for the Star Wars roleplaying game (Fantasy Flight Games).

You are given a QUESTION, the SOURCE PASSAGE it was written from, the EXPECTED ANSWER taken from that passage, and the assistant's REPLY.

Grade only whether the reply states the fact the question asks for:
- CORRECT: the reply states the expected fact (wording may differ; numbers must match).
- PARTIAL: the reply states part of the fact, or states it hedged among alternatives, or is right but adds a materially wrong detail about the same fact.
- WRONG: the reply states a different value or rule for the same fact.
- NOT-FOUND: the reply says it could not find the answer, or answers without addressing the fact.

Ignore citations, formatting and extra correct material. Give a one-line reason.

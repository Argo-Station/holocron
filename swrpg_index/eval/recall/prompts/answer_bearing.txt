You check whether one page of a Star Wars roleplaying game rulebook (Fantasy Flight Games) states a given fact.

You are given a QUESTION, the EXPECTED ANSWER to it, and the text of ONE PAGE. The page may come from any book of the game. Its text was machine-extracted: it may be garbled, out of order, or missing dice symbols.

Decide what this page says about the fact the question asks for:
- carries: the page states the expected answer. Wording may differ; numbers must match. The page must state it: a fact you would have to infer does not carry.
- conflicts: someone who asked this question and relied on this page would come away with a different answer. The page gives a different number, limit, number of uses, cost, skill or rule for the same game element (the same item, weapon, talent, Force power, action or rule). It counts even when the page describes that element in other words than the question, or without a detail the question mentions.
- absent: the page does not answer the question. This includes a page on the same topic that never gives the fact, a number or rule for a different element, and a page about a different character, creature, ship, place or adventure than the one the question means (two characters carrying different gear do not conflict).

Use only the page. Do not use outside knowledge of the game. Give a one-line reason.

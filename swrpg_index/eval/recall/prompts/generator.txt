You write test questions for a search engine over the Star Wars roleplaying game rulebooks (Fantasy Flight Games: Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny, and their supplements and adventures).

You are given ONE passage from one book. Write ONE question that:
- a player or a Game Master at the table would naturally ask;
- is answered by one specific fact stated in this passage (a number, a name, a rule clause, a statistic, a cost, a difficulty, a skill);
- can be answered from this passage alone;
- asks for the fact MOST PARTICULAR to this passage: a named character, creature, vehicle, item, place, talent, event or table entry that this passage describes. Avoid a general rule of the game that every rulebook repeats (how much a stimpack heals, what an extra maneuver costs) unless the passage offers nothing more particular;
- makes sense to someone who has NOT seen the passage: name the subject explicitly, and never write "this character", "this adventure", "that ship", "the above" or "the passage";
- does not name the book or product it comes from, its chapter or its page;
- is written in YOUR OWN WORDS. Do not copy phrasing from the passage: the question must not share any run of 4 or more consecutive words with the passage. Proper names and game terms may appear, but rephrase everything around them.

The answer is that fact, as short as possible (a number, a name, or a phrase of at most 12 words), in the passage's own words.

If the passage is unreadable (garbled text, symbol soup), or is only credits, an index, a table of contents or a legal notice, or states no fact a player could ask about, set "unquestionable" to true, give a short reason, and leave "question" and "answer" empty.

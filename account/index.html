PRIVACY & ACCOUNT

Everything the suite knows about you fits in one sentence. An account id, which provider you signed in with, and a display name.

What we store

  • An account id: ours, random, meaning nothing anywhere else.
  • Your sign-in provider and its opaque subject id: how Google or Discord tells us "same person as last time." It is not your username, address, or profile.
  • A display name, so the bar can say who's signed in.

That is the whole table. No analytics ride on it.

What we never store

  • Your email: we don't ask for it. Sign-in requests only your id and display name. There is a second guard behind that: your provider may transmit your email during sign-in; we discard it. It is never stored.
  • Passwords: none exist here. You sign in with a provider you already trust, so there is nothing to phish, reset, or leak.
  • Provider tokens: the credentials a provider hands back after sign-in. We drop them on arrival instead of saving them.
  • Your profile picture: Google offers one. We throw the link away rather than keep a picture of you we have no use for.
  • Your IP address, or which browser you used: sign-in software normally records both on every session. Ours blanks them, so there is no log of where you signed in from.

The last two used to be stored, and were not listed above. We found them during testing, before a single person other than the developer had an account, and removed them rather than add them to the list.

Signing in is never required

Every tool works signed out, free, forever: characters, GM tools, dice, all of it. An account exists for exactly one purpose, keeping your work with you across devices. Nothing from the books is ever gated behind it.

What travels with your account

Your characters, the character you are part-way through building, your custom NPCs and the groups you put them in, your ships, which books you have switched on, your game variants, and the characters you have deleted (so a deletion on one device is not undone by another).

Two things stay on the device that made them: store shelves and the hold list. Their internal names are not unique across devices yet, so merging them could quietly fold two different shelves into one. They join when that is fixed. Item artwork you import also stays here, because those are picture files rather than records.

Checking…

Delete is immediate and total: the account row and everything attached to it. There is no recovery. Signing in again just makes a fresh, empty account.
